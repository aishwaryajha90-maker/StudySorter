SSC CHSL 🏛️

Explore a central government career after completing Class 12.

Career Overview ✨

SSC CHSL stands for Staff Selection Commission Combined Higher Secondary Level Examination.

It is a competitive examination for recruitment to eligible central government posts.

Job Opportunities 💼

Lower Division Clerk (LDC)

Handles clerical work, records, files and office documentation.

Junior Secretariat Assistant (JSA)

Performs administrative and secretariat-related office duties.

Data Entry Operator (DEO)

Performs data entry and computer-based information processing tasks.

Eligibility 🎓

Educational Qualification: Class 12 pass or equivalent.

Certain DEO posts may require specific subjects such as Science with Mathematics.

Age limits and other requirements depend on the official recruitment notification.

Selection Process 📝

  • Tier-I examination
  • Tier-II examination
  • Typing Test or Skill Test, where applicable
  • Document Verification

The exact selection process is subject to the applicable SSC notification.

Subjects to Prepare 📚

  • English Language
  • General Intelligence and Reasoning
  • Quantitative Aptitude
  • General Awareness

Career Path 📈

Class 12 → SSC CHSL Preparation → Competitive Examination → Selection → Government Service

Official Resources 🔗

Visit the official SSC website for recruitment notifications and examination details.

RRB NTPC 🚆

Explore railway career opportunities through the Non-Technical Popular Categories examination.

Career Overview ✨

RRB NTPC stands for Railway Recruitment Board Non-Technical Popular Categories.

It is a competitive examination conducted for various non-technical posts in Indian Railways.

Job Opportunities 💼

Commercial cum Ticket Clerk

Handles ticketing and commercial responsibilities at railway stations.

Accounts Clerk cum Typist

Performs clerical and accounts-related work with typing responsibilities.

Junior Clerk cum Typist

Handles office records, documentation and clerical work.

Trains Clerk

Performs train-related clerical and operational record duties.

Eligibility 🎓

Undergraduate Posts: Class 12 pass or equivalent.

Some undergraduate posts may require at least 50% marks in Class 12, subject to applicable exceptions.

Graduate Posts: A university degree is required.

Age limits and eligibility conditions depend on the official RRB notification.

Selection Process 📝

  • Computer Based Test (CBT 1)
  • Computer Based Test (CBT 2)
  • Typing Skill Test, where applicable
  • Document Verification
  • Medical Examination

The selection stages vary according to the post and official notification.

Subjects to Prepare 📚

  • Mathematics
  • General Intelligence and Reasoning
  • General Awareness

Career Path 📈

Class 12 → RRB NTPC Preparation → CBT Examinations → Post-specific Tests → Document Verification → Railway Service

Official Resources 🔗

Check the official Railway Recruitment Board websites for recruitment notifications, eligibility and examination updates.

Visit Official RRB Website ↗

Help & FAQs ❔

Frequently Asked Questions

1. How do I use Study Hub?

Open Study Hub from the homepage and choose To-Do List, Timetable, Today's Study Time, or Syllabus Tracker to organize your studies.

2. Is StudySorter free to use?

StudySorter is designed to provide students with free study and organization tools. You don't need to pay to use the available free features.

3. Do I need a student account?

You can explore available features without creating an account. A student account is used for account-based features and study-data syncing.

4. Are my tasks and study progress saved?

Study Hub data is saved in your browser on your device. When cloud syncing is available, your account will help keep your study data accessible across devices.

5. Can I access my study data on another device?

Cross-device syncing is currently being tested. We'll announce when it has been tested and made available.

6. How can I clear my saved study data?

Open Settings and select "Clear saved study data". Read the confirmation message carefully before proceeding.

7. What is the difference between Opinion Section and Your Voice?

Opinion Section is for private feedback that only the StudySorter admin can view. Your Voice is a public space where student posts and names are visible to other users.

8. Where can I find scholarships?

Open Scholarship Finder to explore scholarship information. Always check the official application portal for the latest eligibility rules and deadlines.

9. How can I contact StudySorter?

For questions, suggestions, or assistance, email us at contactstudysorter@gmail.com.
